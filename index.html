pyBBTD#

Split images that have several numbers per pixel into a few parts you can interpret.

Many instruments record more than one number at each pixel: several spectral channels, the polarization of light, or a whole covariance matrix. pyBBTD explains such an image as a sum of a few terms. Each term is a spatial map, showing where it is present, times a signature, describing what it puts in each pixel.
